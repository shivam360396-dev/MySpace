SOMETHING DELICIOUS

My favourite recipe: Veg Sandwich

A quick, colourful snack that is easy to prepare.

Ingredients

  • 4 bread slices
  • 1 small cucumber and 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 boiled potato, sliced
  • Butter or green chutney
  • Salt, pepper, and chaat masala

How to make it

  1. Spread butter or chutney on the bread slices.
  2. Arrange cucumber, tomato, and potato slices.
  3. Sprinkle salt, pepper, and chaat masala.
  4. Cover with another slice and toast if you like.
  5. Cut into triangles and serve fresh.

Tip: Add cheese or mint chutney to make it your own!
